Debt Recovery, Insolvency & Bankruptcy

Recovery and insolvency proceedings for creditors and debtors.

Overview

Unpaid dues and financial distress call for timely, well-chosen action. We act for creditors and for borrowers and guarantors in recovery and insolvency proceedings.

Matters we handle

  • Recovery applications before Debts Recovery Tribunals
  • Proceedings under the SARFAESI Act, 2002
  • Insolvency applications by financial and operational creditors
  • Personal guarantor insolvency
  • Demand notices and pre-litigation recovery
  • Settlement negotiations

How we approach them

For creditors, we help select the remedy that best fits the debt and the debtor. For borrowers and guarantors, we focus on understanding exposure early and on options for settlement or restructuring.

  1. Understand the matter

    We listen, review your documents and identify the facts and deadlines that matter.

  2. Set out your options

    We explain the available routes, their likely costs and timelines, and their risks, in plain language.

  3. Act, and keep you informed

    Once you decide, we act promptly and tell you what has happened at each stage.

This page describes our work in general terms and is not legal advice. Every matter depends on its own facts.
